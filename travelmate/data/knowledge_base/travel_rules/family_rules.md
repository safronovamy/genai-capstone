# Family Travel Rules

---

[RULE_CATEGORY]
[FAMILY_TRAVEL]
[TRAVEL_DOMAIN: pacing]

## Young Children Pacing Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip + child_under_6

[RULES]

- Avoid more than 2 major attractions per day.
- Add a rest break after 2-3 hours of walking or sightseeing.
- Avoid long transportation-heavy schedules.
- Prefer nearby attractions within the same district.
- Reduce excessive queue-heavy activities.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Use parks, cafes, and playgrounds as recovery points between activities.
- Prefer shorter walking routes with flexible pacing.
- Avoid overloaded sightseeing days.

## Comfort and Accessibility Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip

[RULES]

- Prefer attractions with toilets, food options, and stroller-friendly access.
- Avoid excessive uphill walking with small children.
- Prefer attractions with indoor backup options during unstable weather.
- Include opportunities for unstructured rest and snacks.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Add flexible free time blocks between major attractions.
- Avoid tightly packed schedules with continuous walking.

## Evening Activity Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip + child_under_6

[RULES]

- Avoid late evening activities for young children.
- Reduce physically demanding activities after dinner.
- Prefer calm evening walks or early return to accommodation.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Schedule major attractions earlier in the day.
- Use evenings for light activities and recovery.

## Slow Pace Family Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip + slow_pace

[RULES]

- Include one main attraction in the morning and one light activity in the afternoon.
- Avoid district-heavy itineraries.
- Prefer parks, playgrounds, and open areas over crowded tourist routes.
- Reduce the total number of transportation segments.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Use slower transitions between activities.
- Prioritize comfort and reduced fatigue over attraction quantity.

## Family Weather Adaptation Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip + rainy_weather

[RULES]

- Prioritize indoor museums, aquariums, toy stores, and covered attractions.
- Avoid long outdoor walks during rain or cold wind.
- Keep indoor backup activities available near the current district.
- Reduce outdoor waiting time.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Replace weather-sensitive attractions with indoor alternatives.
- Increase indoor recovery and snack opportunities during bad weather.

## Family Budget Rules

[CONDITION]
family_trip + low_budget

[RULES]

- Prioritize playgrounds, parks, beaches, and public spaces.
- Avoid excessive transportation-heavy schedules.
- Prefer free or low-cost child-friendly attractions.
- Reduce unnecessary premium activities.

[ITINERARY_BEHAVIOR]

- Use outdoor public spaces as major itinerary anchors during good weather.
- Prefer flexible schedules over expensive tightly packed itineraries.

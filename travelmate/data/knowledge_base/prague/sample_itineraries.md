## Slow Pace Family Trip

Recommended for families with young children who prefer relaxed schedules and minimal fatigue.

Day 1:
- Morning: Prague Castle
- Afternoon: Relaxed Old Town walk
- Evening: Early dinner and rest

Day 2:
- Morning: Prague Zoo
- Afternoon: Park or playground
- Evening: Riverside walk

Day 3:
- Morning: Museum or indoor activity
- Afternoon: Light shopping or family-friendly cafe
- Evening: Rest

### Heuristics

- Avoid more than 2 major attractions per day.
- Add regular snack and toilet access for children.
- Prefer nearby attractions within the same district.
- Include rest periods after long walking activities.

## Rainy Family Weather Pattern

Recommended for rainy days or cold seasons with children.

### Guidance

- Prioritize museums, aquariums, toy stores, and indoor attractions.
- Reduce long walking distances.
- Add more indoor breaks for children.
- Avoid exposed viewpoints and long outdoor queues.
- Use cafes or restaurants as recovery points between activities.

### Suggested Attractions

- Technical Museum
- Lego Museum Prague
- Chocolate Museum
- Hamleys Toy Store
- Aquapalace Prague

## Summer Low Budget Family Pattern

Recommended for warm weather and budget-conscious families.

### Guidance

- Prioritize parks, playgrounds, riverside walks, and free attractions.
- Avoid intense walking during midday heat.
- Use shaded parks and riverside areas for breaks.
- Schedule indoor museums during the hottest hours.

### Suggested Attractions

- Letna Park
- Kampa Park
- Children's Island
- Old Town Square
- Vltava River Walk

## Active Adult Cultural Trip

Recommended for adults focused on culture, architecture, and historical exploration.

Day 1:
- Morning: National Museum Prague
- Afternoon: Jewish Quarter
- Evening: Cafe Louvre or another historic cafe

Day 2:
- Morning: Prague Castle
- Afternoon: Charles Bridge and Old Town Square
- Evening: Letna Beer Garden

Day 3:
- Morning: Municipal House
- Afternoon: Relaxed city walk or cafe
- Evening: Restaurant-focused free time

### Heuristics

- Combine nearby historical districts to reduce transportation overhead.
- Use cafes and restaurants as intentional cultural experiences.
- Prioritize architectural landmarks during daylight hours.

## Premium Relaxed Prague Pattern

Recommended for travelers prioritizing comfort and premium experiences.

### Guidance

- Reduce attraction density.
- Add higher-quality dining and comfortable rest periods.
- Prefer scenic activities over high-intensity schedules.
- Use indoor premium attractions during bad weather.

### Suggested Attractions

- Aquapalace Prague
- Boat Ride on Vltava
- Cafe Louvre
- Prague Castle private tours

# Prague City Guide

[CITY_GUIDE]
[CITY: Prague]
[COUNTRY: Czech Republic]

## City Overview

Prague is a historic European city known for architecture, walkable districts, castles, parks, museums, and family-friendly public spaces. The city is generally suitable for slow travel because many attractions are concentrated in central districts.

Prague is especially attractive for:
- families with children
- history and architecture lovers
- relaxed city trips
- low and medium budget travel
- walking-focused itineraries

---

## Best Areas for Travelers

### Old Town

Best for:
- first-time visitors
- historic atmosphere
- cafes and restaurants
- walkable sightseeing

Key attractions:
- Old Town Square
- Charles Bridge
- Lego Museum
- Chocolate Museum

Notes:
- Can become crowded during midday and weekends.
- Good area for evening walks and dining.

---

### Castle District

Best for:
- historical sightseeing
- panoramic views
- photography

Key attractions:
- Prague Castle
- surrounding gardens and viewpoints

Notes:
- Hilly area with stairs.
- Less stroller-friendly than central districts.

---

### Letna

Best for:
- parks
- relaxed walking
- scenic viewpoints
- adults and evening activities

Key attractions:
- Letna Park
- Technical Museum
- Letna Beer Garden

Notes:
- Very pleasant during spring and summer.
- Outdoor-focused district.

---

### Petrin Hill

Best for:
- families
- playgrounds
- green areas
- relaxed half-day activities

Key attractions:
- Petrin Park
- Mirror Maze

Notes:
- Suitable for slower travel pace.
- Better during good weather.

---

### Smichov

Best for:
- families with children
- playgrounds
- local atmosphere

Key attractions:
- Children's Island

Notes:
- Less tourist-heavy than Old Town.
- Good for rest breaks with children.

---

## Family Travel Guidance

[FAMILY_GUIDE]

Prague is generally family-friendly and suitable for travel with young children.

Recommended:
- parks and playgrounds
- zoo visits
- short walking routes
- indoor museums during bad weather
- regular cafe and toilet breaks

Avoid:
- overloaded schedules
- long uphill walking segments
- too many district changes in one day

Recommended pace:
- 1-2 major attractions per day for families with children under 6

## Seasonal Recommendations

[SEASONALITY]

### Spring

Best for:
- walking routes
- parks
- castle visits
- riverside walks

Advantages:
- comfortable weather
- fewer crowds than summer

---

### Summer

Best for:
- outdoor parks
- beer gardens
- evening walks
- boat rides

Risks:
- crowded central districts
- midday heat in exposed areas

Recommendations:
- use parks and riverside areas for breaks
- avoid excessive walking during midday

---

### Autumn

Best for:
- museums
- cultural tourism
- cafes
- quieter city atmosphere

Advantages:
- balanced weather
- fewer tourists

---

### Winter

Best for:
- museums
- cafes
- indoor attractions
- Christmas markets

Recommendations:
- prioritize indoor activities during cold weather
- reduce long outdoor walking sessions

---

## Transportation Notes

[TRANSPORT]

Prague is highly walkable in central districts.

Recommended:
- walking
- tram system for longer distances

Notes:
- some historic districts are hilly
- cobblestones may be uncomfortable for strollers in certain areas
- avoid planning too many distant districts in one day

---

## Itinerary Planning Heuristics

[PLANNING_RULES]

- Group nearby attractions together.
- Avoid crossing the city multiple times in one day.
- Add indoor backup options for weather-sensitive days.
- For family trips, include breaks every 2-3 hours.
- Use cafes and parks as recovery points between attractions.
- Prefer flexible schedules over dense attraction lists.

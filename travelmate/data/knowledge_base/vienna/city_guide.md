[CITY: Vienna]
[COUNTRY: Austria]

## City Overview

Vienna is a historic European capital known for imperial architecture, museums, classical music culture, parks, cafes, and high quality public transportation. The city is clean, organized, and highly suitable for both family travel and relaxed cultural tourism.

Vienna is especially attractive for:
- families with children
- museum and culture lovers
- relaxed city trips
- architecture-focused tourism
- slow and medium pace travel
- cafe and food culture

The city combines large green areas with dense historical districts, making it flexible for different travel styles and weather conditions.

## Family Travel Guidance

[FAMILY_GUIDE]

Vienna is highly suitable for family travel with young children.

Recommended:
- parks and gardens
- zoo visits
- interactive museums
- short walking routes
- indoor attractions during bad weather

Advantages:
- reliable public transportation
- clean public infrastructure
- many stroller-friendly areas
- good availability of cafes and toilets

Avoid:
- overloaded museum schedules
- excessive district changes in one day
- long outdoor walks during bad weather

Recommended pace:
- 1-2 major attractions per day for families with young children

## Itinerary Planning Heuristics

[PLANNING_RULES]

- Group attractions by district.
- Avoid excessive transportation between activities.
- Add indoor backup options for weather-sensitive days.
- For family trips, include regular rest opportunities.
- Use cafes and parks as recovery points between major attractions.
- Prefer flexible schedules over overloaded itineraries.
